Common failures:
  - No node with available nvidia.com/gpu (the chart requests {{ .Values.gpuCount }}).
  - PVC unbound: the chosen storageClass does not exist or has no capacity.
  - CrashLoopBackOff during startup: the ai-dock image needs CUDA at boot.
    Setting gpuEnabled=false in production is not supported.
